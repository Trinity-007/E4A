## Getting Started

### Step 1: Create Your Account
- Go to `signup.html` or click **Sign Up** on the homepage

### Step 2: Access the Sell Page
- Click the **"Sell"** link in the navigation menu on any page

## 📝 Listing Your Item

### Required Information

**1. Product Name**
- Be specific and descriptive (e.g., "iPhone 12 Pro Max - 128GB - Space Gray")
- Maximum 100 characters
- Include brand and key features

**2. Price**
- Enter in Nigerian Naira (₦)
- Minimum: ₦100
- Maximum: ₦10,000,000
- Tip: Price competitively compared to similar items

**3. Category**
Select from:
- 📱 Phones & Mobile Devices
- 👗 Fashion & Clothing
- 💻 Computers & Laptops
- 🎧 Electronics & Gadgets
- 👜 Accessories
- 🏠 Home & Living
- 🍳 Kitchen & Dining
- 🛋️ Furniture
- 🔧 Tools & Hardware
- 🚗 Vehicles & Auto
- 🔌 Appliances
- 📦 Other

**4. Condition**
Choose one:
- **Like New** - Never used, in original packaging
- **Very Good** - Minimal signs of use
- **Good** - Normal wear and tear
- **Fair** - Visible signs of use/minor damage
- **Vintage/Antique** - Older items with character

**5. Description**
- Minimum 20 characters
- Be honest and detailed
- Include:
  - How long you've owned it
  - Any wear, scratches, or damage
  - Dimensions and weight (if relevant)
  - Functionality - does everything work?
  - Original packaging/accessories included?
  - Reason for selling

**6. Product Image**
- One main photo of your item
- Supported formats: JPG, PNG, WebP
- Maximum file size: 5MB
- Tips:
  - Use good lighting
  - Show the item clearly from multiple angles mentally
  - Include any damage or wear
  - Take photo against plain background

## 💡 Pro Tips for Sellers

### Title & Description Tips
✅ **Good Example:**
```
iPhone 12 Pro Max 128GB Space Gray - Excellent Condition

Owned for 8 months. Minimal use, always in case. 
No scratches on screen or body. All features work perfectly.
Includes original box, charger, and EarPods.
Selling due to upgrade to iPhone 14.
```

❌ **Avoid:**
```
iphone - good condition - must sell
```

### Pricing Tips
- Research similar items on E4A Marketplace
- Consider condition and age
- Factor in buyer protection guarantee
- Set fair prices to attract more buyers
- Be prepared to negotiate with offers

### Photography Tips
- Use natural daylight or bright indoor lighting
- Clean the item before photographing
- Show any damage honestly
- Take photos from multiple angles (mentally)
- No filters or heavy editing
- Include a photo with and without packaging

### Description Accuracy
✅ Always mention:
- Exact condition
- Any defects or damage
- Age of the item
- Original accessories included
- Warranty status (if any)
- Reason for selling

## 🛍️ After Your Item is Listed

### What Happens Next

1. **Item Goes Live** (within minutes)
   - Appears in marketplace search
   - Visible to all E4A buyers
   - Listed under your selected category

2. **Buyers Can Browse**
   - View your item details
   - See your photos and description
   - Check your seller rating

3. **Offers & Negotiations**
   - Buyers can make offers below asking price
   - You can accept, reject, or counter-offer
   - Agree on final price

4. **Complete the Sale**
   - Once buyer accepts price, arrange payment
   - Agree on delivery/pickup method
   - Complete transaction

5. **Build Your Reputation**
   - Get positive reviews from happy buyers
   - Earn seller badges and verification
   - Increase sales over time

## 📞 Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: How much does it cost to sell?**
A: Listing is FREE! No fees or commissions on E4A Marketplace.

**Q: How long will my listing stay active?**
A: Listings stay active until sold or you remove them.

**Q: Can I edit my listing after posting?**
A: You can modify descriptions and price through your account.

**Q: What if no one buys my item?**
A: You can relist, adjust the price, or improve the photos.

**Q: How do I get paid?**
A: Arrange payment directly with the buyer (cash, bank transfer, etc.).

**Q: Can I sell internationally?**
A: Currently E4A is for Nigeria. International shipping available by arrangement.

**Q: What if a buyer is not satisfied?**
A: Handle disputes professionally. E4A encourages fair resolution.

**Q: How do I become a verified seller?**
A: Build a good history, get positive reviews, and complete your seller verification.
